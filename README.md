# Customer-Segmentation-Purchasing-Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer data from a marketing campaign to better understand customer characteristics, purchasing behavior, spending patterns, website activity, and campaign responses.

The project covers the complete data analysis process, including data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, data visualization, identification of key insights, and the development of business recommendations. The analysis was conducted using Google Sheets.

## Dataset
The dataset used in this project is the Customer Personality Analysis dataset, which contains customer demographic information, purchasing behavior, website activity, and responses to marketing campaigns. The dataset was obtained from Kaggle and analyzed using Google Sheets.

## Data Cleaning
During the data cleaning process, I identified and corrected several data quality issues, including:

- Duplicate records
- Missing values
- Inconsistent categorical values
- Incorrect birth-year data

 I also standardized customer education and marital status categories to make the data more consistent and suitable for analysis.

 ## Exploratory Data Analisys
 After completing the data cleaning process, the dataset was ready for exploratory data analysis. The EDA focused on identifying patterns and trends within the customer data through descriptive analysis and data visualization.

 ## Key Insights
 The analysis revealed key insights related to customer spending, purchasing behavior, website activity, and marketing campaign responses. 

- **Income & Spending:** A strong positive correlation of **0.79** was found between income and total spending, indicating that customers with higher incomes tend to spend more.
- **Marketing Campaign Response:** Customers who responded to the campaigns had an average total spending of **987.39**, compared with **539.45** among non-responders, approximately **83% higher**.
- **Purchasing Channels:** The analysis of purchasing channels revealed differences in how customers choose to shop, helping identify the most relevant channels for the customer base.
- **Web Behavior:** Website visits did not show a clear relationship with web purchases. The higher-visit groups contained very few customers, limiting the reliability of conclusions for those segments.
- **Customer Spending by Marital Status:** Customers classified as **Widow** showed the highest average spending among the three marital-status groups, although this result should be considered alongside the size of each group.

 These findings provide a clearer understanding of customer behavior and highlight areas that could support future business decisions.

 ## Conclusions
 The analysis provided a clearer understanding of customer characteristics, purchasing behavior, spending patterns, and marketing campaign responses. Income showed a strong relationship with total spending, while campaign responders displayed substantially higher average spending than non-responders.

 The analysis also showed that website visit frequency alone does not necessarily translate into more web purchases, highlighting the importance of looking beyond traffic volume when evaluating digital customer behavior.

 ## Business Recommendations
 Based on the findings of the analysis, the following recommendations could help improve customer targeting and marketing strategies:

- **Segment customers by income and spending behavior:** Use income and spending patterns to create customer segments and develop more targeted offers and promotions.
- **Prioritize high-value customer segments:** Customers with higher spending levels could be targeted with personalized offers, loyalty programs, and retention strategies.
- **Optimize marketing campaigns:** Since campaign responders showed significantly higher average spending, further analysis could be used to identify the characteristics of customers who are more likely to respond to future campaigns.
- **Improve digital conversion:** Since higher website visit frequency did not necessarily result in more web purchases, the company could investigate the customer journey and identify potential barriers between website visits and purchases.
- **Use purchasing channel insights:** Understanding which purchasing channels are most frequently used can help the company allocate resources and develop strategies according to customer preferences.
